Loading states — skeletons and buffering (approved 2026-10-30)

Drawn from @quran/design-tokens and the apple-design skill (ADR 0010). A skeleton keeps the real screen's header, search, filters and tabs, and swaps only the content that is still loading for placeholder bars, shaped like the rows they stand for. It is the Tafsir frame's placeholder bar made into one component: fillSecondary, height 10 (12 for a name, 14 for a Mushaf line), radius half the height; tiles and buttons keep their real size and radius. On the brand card it uses onBrand at 20%, the card's own track colour.
Motion: the bars pulse together between opacity 1 and 0.55, duration-pulse (1,000 ms) each way, ease-in-out, repeating. With Reduce Motion on they stay still at 1. A loading region is one accessibility element, «جارٍ التحميل» with busy, and its bars are hidden from VoiceOver and TalkBack. Content replaces the skeleton with a duration-fast cross-fade.
Where: the Listen tab before the first catalogue sync (it used to say «no reciters»), Home and the index while the content database opens on first launch, the tafsir panel while a tafsir loads, the reciter page while its pack installs (the index row skeleton, without the medallion), and a page-exact Mushaf page while its font loads from the CDN (the Quran text is never drawn in another font meanwhile). Search shows nothing until the database is ready, as now.
Buffering: while the player is loading, the play/pause button shows pause with a thin ring that turns once per second (the mini bars draw the same ring at their size). With Reduce Motion on, the ring stays still and pulses like a skeleton.

Tablet reader — expanded and medium windows (approved 2026-10-27)

Drawn from @quran/design-tokens and the apple-design skill (ADR 0010), for approval before Phase 2. The reader is a full-window screen above the tabs, so the sidebar is hidden while reading (content first, as in Apple Books).
Expanded (840 and up): ayah actions and tafsir open in a persistent side panel (380 pt) on the trailing edge. Tapping an ayah fills the panel instead of opening a sheet. The page stays centred in the space beside the panel and never exceeds 640 pt (spec §4). The background extends under the panel, and the panel is the glass layer. The toolbar toggle hides or shows the panel.
Narrowing: following the HIG rule that tertiary columns hide first, the panel docks only when page and panel both fit (window ≥ 1,100 pt). From 840 to 1,099 pt it floats over the page, and a tap outside closes it.
Medium (600–839): one centred column with ayah actions as a large sheet (formSheet on iPad, a bottom sheet on Android foldables).
All sizes: touch targets are at least 44 pt; Reduce Transparency swaps glass for solid glassFallback; the mini-player stays in the reading area, never under the panel.

Revised for ADR 0012 (Apple HIG), 2026-10-27. The layout approved above is unchanged. The Mushaf frames here show Madani page-exact (QCF), so the recited ayah gets the margin rule; the phone Mushaf frame shows Uthmani Hafs, where the app draws the marker and fills it. The selected or recited ayah is a neutral highlight-active wash, never gold. In page-exact Mushaf the ayah marker is a font glyph and is never recoloured; a brand rule in the page margin beside each line of the ayah marks it instead. In Reading mode the marker fills with brand and a leading brand rule marks the block. The top edge is back plus one glass group (bookmark, reader settings, ayah actions, panel); the title is plain text over a scroll edge fade. Highlight swatches show the choice with an ink ring and a check. The mini-player line fills from the leading edge (right in Arabic).
