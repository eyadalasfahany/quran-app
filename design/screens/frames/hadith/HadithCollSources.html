السيرة النبوية — Seerah map and timeline (proposed 2026-11-14)

Phase 8, ADR 0089 (Proposed); plan docs/plans/phase-8.md «Frames». Pre-approved for the overnight build (Revision 2), still captioned «(بانتظار الموافقة)» until the developer looks. Map art is our own SVG in the seerah folder of the shared design assets (coast from Natural Earth, public domain; relief drawn by hand), with drawn positions in layout-*.json beside it. Event titles on the map are mock headings and every paragraph is a bracketed placeholder: the real titles and text are the book's own («نور اليقين»، الخضري). Counts are UI mocks. The timeline is the Prophet's ﷺ age, year by year from 0 to 63 (developer, 14 Nov 2026), with life-stage bands (الطفولة ٠–١١، الصبا ١٢–١٩، الشباب ٢٠–٣٩، الكهولة ٤٠–٦٣) and milestone marks (المولد، الزواج من خديجة، نزول الوحي، الهجرة، الوفاة); the Hijri year is only a small secondary detail on the event page (convention age 53 = 1 هـ, open as Q6). The map is geography and is never mirrored in English; only the chrome and the timeline follow the reading direction.
