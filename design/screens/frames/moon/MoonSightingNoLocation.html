نقاط الدخول — Menu cards and the Home date line (proposed 2026-11-02)

Proposed under ADR 0043. The three new cards use the approved Menu card style (ADR 0032). Names appears only while the names pack is installed. The Home date line is an addition to the approved Home frame and needs approval; it opens the Hijri calendar. The Salah header already shows the Hijri date (ADR 0030 §6); whether the adjustment applies there is a decision for the developer.
