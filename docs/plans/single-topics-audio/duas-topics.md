# Duas topics, one per dua — review sheet

Status: **reviewed** (developer, 15 Nov 2026: every row marked reviewed, ADR 0092). Drafted by Claude on 15 Nov 2026 for ADR 0092 step 2, replacing the keyword-rule draft in `docs/plans/azkar-duas-split/topics.md`. Each Quranic dua was decided from the verse's meaning and each hadith chapter from its OpenITI title. Every key has one topic from `config/azkar_topics.php`, or «—»: no topic, so it stays only in its source category («من القرآن الكريم» or its hadith book). Config: `apps/api/config/duas_topics.php` (keys and topic ids only).

Conventions: Tirmidhi chapters titled «باب منه» follow the titled chapter before them; untitled «باب» chapters get no topic. Nasa'i book 50 is all «الاستعاذة من …»: «التحصين» unless the title names a listed subject (debt, distress, provision, enemies). Where a Hisn al-Muslim chapter covers the same act, the same topic is used (noted «كحصن المسلم N»).
