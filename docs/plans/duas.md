## Risks

- **A wrong Quranic dua list is a religious error.** Nothing reaches production unreviewed; the generator is recall-first; its false positives (narrative quotations) and misses (supplications without «رب») are named in the sheet; the reviewer adds by hand.
- **HadeethEnc terms: "no deletion".** Showing a subset may count as deletion (decision 6). Hiding the grade under ADR 0042 D4 may too (decision 7).
- **HadeethEnc is `granted` by developer decision, with no written terms** (ADR 0044 §2). One more feature depends on it.
- **The snapshot is git-ignored** (2.5 MB, 207 files). Without decision 11, production has no Prophetic duas; parts 1, 2, 4 and 5 are unaffected.
- **Phase 7 dependency.** Part 2 and the collection link rows need `ft/hadith` merged; Phase 7 is not started (its plan, status line). It has no switch (decision 20, removed 2 Nov 2026, ADR 0045). Its source may switch to Sunnah.com, changing numbers: book numbers in `collection_books` and `dua_collection_refs` must follow `hadith_ref_aliases` or be regenerated and re-reviewed.
- **Mixed content in the hadith books.** The five books contain narrations about dua, not only duas; the row is labelled «الدعاء في كتب الحديث» (books), not «أدعية».
- **Shared files with Phase 6, Phase 7 and ADR 0045:** `ResourceGroup`, `PackSchema`, `content-tables.ts`, Drizzle migration numbers, `clear.ts`, Menu and Home frames, i18n files. Add at the end of lists; take the next free migration number at merge; one author per frame.
- **ADR numbering.** `docs/adr/` has two 0042s and two 0043s; this plan cites ADR 0044 and 0045 by the developer's numbers, with file names.
- **Hadith explanation is long.** The detail scrolls; the first screen shows the text, the narrator line and the link card.
- **Duplicates with Azkar.** 9 hadiths link to Azkar entries; others may repeat an Azkar text the 40-character rule missed. The reviewer adds links.
