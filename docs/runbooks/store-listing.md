# Store listing — Taybah 1.0 (App Store and Google Play, Arabic and English)

Copy to paste into App Store Connect and the Google Play Console. Arabic is the primary language on both stores; English is the second localization. Every field is within its store limit (checked by script; counts in brackets).

Rules this copy follows:
- Reciters: production serves 20 recitations by 17 distinct reciters (al-Husary, Abdul Basit and al-Minshawi each have two). The copy says 17 reciters.
- Only features in the 1.0 build are claimed. No hadith collections, no translations of the Quran, no Adhan audio, no Azkar audio, no AI. Azkar is listed because it must be live before release (see `app-review-notes.md`, Part C). If Azkar is not live, remove every Azkar line, including the name.
- No ayah is quoted: App Store Connect rejected the Uthmani script in the description (2 Nov 2026). Quran text is never typed or re-encoded to work around this.
- No ranking claims ("best", "#1"), no emoji, no competitor names, no other platform's name: both stores reject these.
- Sources are credited at the end of each description (content plan: credits in Settings and in the store description).

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

Taybah is one of the names of the Prophet's city, al-Madinah. The app carries that name and that purpose: a calm daily companion for reading, hearing and reflecting on the Book of Allah, with no ads and no distractions.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best of you are those who learn the Quran and teach it." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

THE MUSHAF
• The Madinah Mushaf, page by page and line by line, as printed by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex.
• The Quran text is kept exactly as received from its source, not a letter changed. All 6,236 ayahs are verified before every release.
• Opens instantly and works offline.
• Surah and juz index, and fast word search across the Quran that ignores diacritics.
• Focus mode for reading and memorization, with a self-test for revision.

TAFSIR
• Nine classical Arabic tafsirs: al-Muyassar, Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi, al-Baghawi, al-Sa'di, al-Jalalayn, al-Wasit and Ibn Ashur's al-Tahrir wa al-Tanwir.
• Shown as their authors wrote them, credited, never shortened or rewritten.
• Download the ones you want and read them offline.

RECITATION
• 17 renowned reciters, including Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary, Muhammad Siddiq al-Minshawi, Abdul Basit Abdul Samad, Mishary Alafasy, Maher al-Muaiqly, Abdul Rahman al-Sudais, Saud al-Shuraim, Saad al-Ghamdi and Yasser al-Dosari.
• Follow the recited word on the mushaf page as the reciter reads, to recite along and perfect your pronunciation.
• Ayah repeat for memorization, a sleep timer, and playback in the background and from the lock screen.
• Download surahs and listen offline.

PRAYER AND QIBLA
• Prayer times for your location using the Umm al-Qura method, calculated on your device. Your location is never sent to a server.
• Prayer time notifications.
• Qibla compass, or choose your city by hand if you prefer not to share your location.

AZKAR AND TASBIH
• Morning, evening and daily Azkar from Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim).
• A counter for every dhikr with a set number of repetitions, a done mark for each dhikr, favourites, and reminders at the times of the Azkar.
• A digital tasbih for your daily glorification.

YOUR DAILY WIRD
• A Khatma plan that divides the Quran over the days you choose.
• Daily goals for your wird and tasbih, with a gentle reading reminder.
• Bookmarks, highlights and notes on ayahs.

CRAFTED WITH CARE
• Arabic first, with a complete English interface.
• Light and dark themes, with an automatic switch to dark after Maghrib.
• Works on iPhone and iPad, with a layout that fits every screen.

YOUR PRIVACY IS AN AMANAH
• No account, no sign-in.
• No ads, no tracking.
• Your bookmarks, notes and Khatma stay on your device alone.

Taybah is free. We ask Allah to accept it as a sadaqah jariyah and to make it of benefit.

### Play Console declarations

- **Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK).** Taybah plays Quran recitation and audio with the screen off, through a media-playback foreground service (expo-audio background playback). Play requires a short video showing the feature; record the playback-with-screen-off flow and paste its link into the Play Console foreground service declaration.
- **Data safety.** No Firebase remains in the 1.0 build (ADR 0058), so the SDK list supports "No data collected". Location stays on the device.
