## Decision (proposed)

### 1. Five parts under one section, «الأدعية»

1. **الأدعية القرآنية:** supplications in the Quran, stored as verse-key ranges.
2. **الدعاء في كتب الحديث:** the books of supplication of the Phase 7 collections (ADR 0042). Navigation only: a list of `<collection slug>:<book number>` rows that open Phase 7's book screen. No hadith is copied or imported.
3. **الأدعية النبوية:** hadiths from HadeethEnc's dua and dhikr categories, with HadeethEnc's explanation (section 3).
4. **حصن المسلم:** a view of the imported Azkar entries by topic. No new content.
5. **أدعيتي:** the user's own duas, local only.

Parts 2 and 4 import nothing: part 2 reuses the `hadith_*` rows and screens of Phase 7, part 4 the `azkar_*` rows and the Azkar reader. Every part is shown only when its pack is installed (ADR 0017: no dead ends).

### 1a. Phase 7 collections first; why HadeethEnc is still proposed

The Phase 7 collections are the hadith source the app already has (ADR 0042), so the Duas section uses them first and adds no second copy of any collection hadith:

- **Part 2** lists the five books of supplication above (652 entries). The list is a fact about the source (each book's own title names supplication or seeking refuge), not a selection of hadiths by us. The book titles shown are Phase 7's (`config/hadith_titles.php`: Arabic when approved, «كتاب {n}» otherwise, ADR 0042 D9). Which books are listed is decision 18.
- **Part 3 links into the collections** wherever a HadeethEnc dua is also a collection hadith (section 3, `dua_collection_refs`), so the reader can open the full narration.

The collections alone do not give a list of duas, for reasons found in the source:

1. A book of supplication is not a list of duas. Its entries are full narrations (104 of 106 in Bukhari's book 80 begin with the chain of narrators), and some are about dua (its virtue, its manners, events) rather than a dua to say. Picking the duas out of them, or cutting a narration down to its supplication, is a religious judgement and an edit of the text; we do neither (non-negotiables 1 and 6 applied to hadith, ADR 0042 "Hadith text is never altered").
2. fawazahmed0 has no باب level (ADR 0042 D8), so inside a 244-entry book nothing says which narration is for which occasion. Any grouping would be ours.
3. Phase 7 stores no explanation. HadeethEnc gives a named publisher's selection, a short matn, an explanation, a reference and benefits for each item.

So HadeethEnc is proposed for part 3 only, as a curated selection by a named publisher, and **part 2 ships without it**. If the developer drops HadeethEnc (decision 19), the section has parts 1, 2, 4 and 5, and release blockers 2, 3 and 5 disappear.

### 2. Quranic duas

- **Stored data:** a `dua_quran_spans` row has `surah`, `verse_from`, `verse_to`. It never holds Quran text. The app renders the verses from `content.db.verses` with Taybah's Quran font and rules (ADR 0031 §4, non-negotiable 1). «فتح في المصحف» opens the Mushaf at `verse_from`.
- **No translation or transliteration** in any interface language (ADR 0014).
- **Provenance.** No named, licensed list exists (Context). So:
  1. A generator, `duas:quran-candidates`, reads the bundled Tanzil simple text (`packages/quran-text-check/data/tanzil-quran-simple-1.1.txt`, the same text `azkar:quran-map-candidates` matches against). It writes `candidate` rows with evidence (the rule that matched). Rules:
     - the verse begins with «ربنا» or «رب», with or without a leading «و»;
     - or it begins with a speech verb («قال», «قالوا», «قل», «يقولون») followed by «ربنا», «رب» or «اللهم»;
     - or it contains «اللهم»;
     - or «ربنا» or «رب» is followed within three words by a supplication imperative from a fixed list.
  2. Measured on Tanzil simple 1.1 on 3 Nov 2026 (`duas:quran-candidates`, after path/surah-key fixes): 33 verses begin with «ربنا» or «رب»; 33 begin with a speech verb plus a vocative; 5 contain «اللهم»; 9 have «ربنا»/«رب» within 3 words of a supplication imperative; **80 total unique candidates** (rule priority, not union). An earlier draft of this paragraph said 78 (rule 4 = 7) from a run that mis-keyed verses; that count is superseded. The still-older manual estimate of 32, 42, 74, 100 used union logic. The rules are **recall-first**: they include narrative quotations and descriptions that are not duas. They also **miss** supplications that use none of these forms (for example 1:6–7). The developer reviews both directions.
  3. Rows are `candidate`, `reviewed`, `rejected` or `added` (a verse the reviewer adds by hand, with the reviewer's evidence). **Production accepts only `reviewed` and `added` rows**, the same rule as `azkar_quran_map` (ADR 0031 §11). Local builds may show `candidate` rows and list them as warnings.
  4. Adjacent verses that form one supplication are one **span** (a row with `verse_from` and `verse_to`). The reviewer sets the span; the generator only proposes single verses.
  5. A printed or published compilation the reviewer owns may be used to **cross-check**. Its text is never copied, and the review sheet names it as the reviewer's evidence. It is not a stored source.
- **Order and grouping:** Mushaf order, labelled by surah name (a fact from `chapters`). **No themes**, because no source provides them and we do not author them. If a named, permitted source gives themes later, they are added with that source as provenance.
- **Audio (amended, 3 Nov 2026, ADR 0055):** no separate audio clips for Quranic duas. The detail screen shows one «فتح في المصحف» pill that opens the Mushaf reader at `verse_from` using the Quran `playerStore` (ADR 0008); the user then taps «play from here» in the reader. The Quran `playerStore` is used, never `azkarPlayerStore`. Ayah timings are shown only if already present for the surah; no timing is fetched or generated. A dedicated verse-range play button is a cut line; revisit if the reader gains a programmatic «play from here» API. Rules are recorded in ADR 0055.

### 3. Prophetic duas from HadeethEnc

- **Source and terms.** HadeethEnc's official API, language `ar`. The terms above apply. The app shows the attribution on every item: «موسوعة الأحاديث النبوية (HadeethEnc.com)» (the string `HadeethEncNotes::SOURCE_NAME` already uses) and the item's `https://hadeethenc.com/ar/browse/hadith/<id>` URL.
- **Categories (proposed).** The textual dua and dhikr categories, by HadeethEnc id: 301 «أذكار الصباح والمساء», 302 «الأذكار المطلقة», 303 and 305 and 306 (enter and leave: home, the toilet, the mosque), 307 «الأذكار التي تقال في أوقات الشدة», 308 «الأذكار للأمور العارضة», 313 «الأدعية المأثورة». Distinct hadiths in them, from the pinned snapshot's list files: **103** (per category: 9, 10, 2, 1, 2, 4, 32, 44; the sum is 104, one hadith sits in two). All 103 have an explanation; 84 share one grade string and the rest have seven others.
- **Not proposed:** the categories about dua and dhikr, not duas themselves: 299, 300 (dhikr), 309 «أحكام الدعاء», 310 «أنواع الدعاء», 311 «آداب الدعاء», 312 «أسباب إجابة الدعاء وموانعه», 278 «فضائل الذكر», 279 «فضل الدعاء». Distinct hadiths in them: 90, nine of which are also in the 103. Whether they appear as a second list «في فضل الدعاء وآدابه» is a developer decision (below). Their category titles come from HadeethEnc.
- **Snapshot and pin.** `duas:snapshot hadeethenc` fetches the listed categories and every distinct hadith once, sequentially and politely, stores the bytes as received and writes `manifest.json` with each file's SHA-256 and the manifest SHA-256. The pin goes in `config/duas.php` (date and manifest SHA-256), as the Azkar pin does. It reuses `HadeethEncSnapshotter` (`app/Domains/Azkar/Sources`); the category ids live in `config/duas.php`. A snapshot that fails its pin is an error.
- **Stored exactly as provided:** `hadeeth` (the text), `title`, `attribution` (narrator and source line), `grade`, `explanation`, `reference` (the takhrij string) and `hints` («الفوائد»). Never trimmed or edited. **HadeethEnc's terms forbid deletion**, so the proposal is to show every non-empty field, including «الفوائد», which ADR 0031 §12 left out of the Azkar notes. This is a decision for the developer (below).
- **Grade.** HadeethEnc's `grade` is shown as provided, with its attribution. HadeethEnc names no grader in `grade`, so the UI shows it as «الدرجة» with the source line and never as Taybah's own ruling. **This conflicts with ADR 0042 §5 (D4)**, which hides every Phase 7 grade until the Sunnah.com phase. The developer decides (decision 7); this ADR does not override ADR 0042 without an explicit exception.
- **Themes** are HadeethEnc's category titles, shown as the group heading of each list. Not authored by us.
- **Dedupe against Hisn al-Muslim.** The existing links file `apps/api/config/azkar_hadeethenc_links.php` holds 14 candidate links, to 9 distinct hadiths (8 of them in the 103). A Prophetic dua whose hadith has a link shows a row «في حصن المسلم · <book number>» that opens the Azkar entry. Its text is not copied. `dua_links` rows hold `hadeeth_id`, `entry_key` and `status` (`candidate` or `reviewed`), ids and evidence only. Links are matched by containment of at least 40 normalised characters (`HadeethEncLinker`, ADR 0031 §12).
- **Reuse of the Hadith collections (ADR 0042).** A prophetic dua that is also in a collection **links to it instead of copying it** via `dua_collection_refs` (`candidate`|`reviewed`, production `reviewed` only). Phase 7 stores no HadeethEnc rows; `dua_hadith` is the only place HadeethEnc records live for Duas.
- **English interface:** Arabic only in the first release. A second snapshot with `language=en` (ADR 0014 allows hadith translations) is a cut-line item.

### 4. Hisn al-Muslim by topic

- The Duas home shows the existing Azkar browse categories (OpenDua's editorial tags, ADR 0031 §9) as topic tiles and a row «حصن المسلم». A tile opens `/azkar/category/<id>` and a row opens `/azkar`. No content is added or copied; the Azkar reader, counter, audio and favourites are unchanged.
- This view appears only when the Azkar pack is installed (ADR 0017: no dead ends).

### 5. «أدعيتي»

- **Local only** in `user.db` (ADR 0027, non-negotiable 4). Additive migration: `my_duas`, `dua_favorites`; both in `PERSONAL_TABLES`. No outbox, no API.
- **Editable** with «من كتابتك» / "Written by you" on every surface. Optional reminders via the reminders hub with a new `my_dua` target type (same budget as Azkar reminders, ADR 0031 §7).
- **Not allowed:** copying sourced duas into «أدعيتي»; use favourites instead.

### 6. Data delivery

- **A new pack group `duas`**, not an extension of `azkar` (separate sources, versioning and locks).
- **MySQL and `content.db` tables:** `dua_items`, `dua_quran_spans`, `dua_hadith`, `dua_groups`, `dua_links`, `dua_collection_refs`, `dua_collection_books` — see plan task 2a for columns.
- **Commands:** `duas:snapshot hadeethenc`, `duas:quran-candidates`, `duas:hadeethenc-links`, `duas:collection-refs`, `duas:import` (`--download`, `--defer-pack`; no env switch, ADR 0042-features-enabled-by-default).
- **Release.** `docker/start-web.sh` pattern (ADR 0044-packs-build-at-web-start): import with `--defer-pack`, then `content:ensure-packs`. **Correctness rules (not switches):** production accepts only `reviewed`/`added` Quranic spans; pinned snapshot; `hadeethenc` source `granted` for prophetic rows.
- **Snapshot on server:** git-ignored (~2.5 MB); release must fetch or build and verify pin (decision 11).

### 7. App

- **Entry points:** «الأدعية» Menu card and Home tile when a `duas` pack is installed (ADR 0017, 0032). Routes `/duas/*`.
- **Frames** (`quran-app-screens.html`, section `duas`): `DuasHome`, `DuaQuranDetail`, `DuaHadithDetail`, `MyDuas`, `MyDuasEmpty`, `MyDuaEditor`, each with `Dark` and `English` variants (17 frames).
- **Share, search, favourites:** as Azkar patterns; Quranic search via `search_quran` + spans only; global search scope chips are a cut line.
- **No switches.** Visibility follows installed packs and reviewed rows only.

## Decisions accepted, 3 Nov 2026

All 21 decisions accepted (developer, 3 Nov 2026). The recommendation from each item in «Decisions that need the developer» is binding. Audio rules are in ADR 0055 (cited here for reference).

1. **Scope and name.** Five parts under «الأدعية»: Quranic, hadith books (navigation only), Prophetic (HadeethEnc), Hisn by topic (Azkar view), and «أدعيتي».
2. **Quranic review.** Developer or named reviewer marks every generator row in `docs/plans/duas/quran-duas-review.md`. Cross-check with a compilation the reviewer owns is permitted.
3. **Quranic provenance.** Generator-and-review model (ADR 0044 §2 rules 1–4). No named licensed list exists; verse keys are a fact, but a named reviewer must stand behind each row. Production accepts only `reviewed` and `added` rows.
4. **Quranic grouping.** Mushaf order, surah labels, no themes (no named source for themes exists).
5. **Prophetic categories.** Eight textual categories (103 hadiths): 301, 302, 303, 305, 306, 307, 308, 313. The 90 virtue/etiquette hadiths are not imported.
6. **HadeethEnc fields.** Show all non-empty fields **except** `words_meanings` (cut line). «الفوائد» is shown. No field is hidden, trimmed or edited (HadeethEnc terms forbid modification).
7. **HadeethEnc grade vs ADR 0042 D4.** Show grade for HadeethEnc-sourced duas only, with HadeethEnc's source line («الدرجة» from HadeethEnc.com). This is a documented exception to ADR 0042 D4's app-wide hide; that exception is limited to the `dua_hadith` table and does not affect Phase 7 hadith grades.
8. **Dedupe.** Keep the prophetic row and add a link row «في حصن المسلم · <number>» when a reviewed `dua_links` match exists. The Azkar text is not copied.
9. **«أدعيتي».** Included, with optional `my_dua` reminders via the reminders hub.
10. **Pack group.** Separate `duas` pack group; not an extension of `azkar`.
11. **Snapshot location.** Same pattern as Azkar: fetched and pin-verified at web start (`docker/start-web.sh`). Snapshot is git-ignored.
12. **Navigation.** «الأدعية» Menu card and Home tile (each only when a `duas` pack is installed, ADR 0017). Hisn topics appear in both Duas home and Azkar; no new content is added.
13. **English hadith.** Arabic only at launch. A second `language=en` snapshot is a cut-line item.
14. **Frames.** 17 frames approved as drawn (2 Nov 2026). Menu and Home deltas to be drawn at task 6; Azkar favourites layout reused for Duas favourites.
15. **Global search.** Later. Section-only search ships first.
16. **HadeethEnc written confirmation.** Ship with Postman terms + `granted` row as already accepted (ADR 0042-features-enabled-by-default §2); pursue written confirmation non-blocking.
17. **Collection refs on prophetic duas.** Yes, reviewed candidates (`dua_collection_refs`). A `reviewed` ref shows «في <collection> · <number>» opening Phase 7's hadith screen.
18. **Hadith books in part 2.** Five books (652 entries): `bukhari` 80, `muslim` 48, `tirmidhi` 48, `ibnmajah` 34, `nasai` 50. `malik` 60 is a cut-line option.
19. **Keep HadeethEnc part 3.** Yes. If the snapshot is delayed at release, ship parts 2, 4 and 5 first; part 3 follows once the snapshot and decisions 6–7 are settled.
20. **Phase 7 hadith switch.** Remove the switch; align ADR 0042 D6 with ADR 0042-features-enabled-by-default. Phase 7 packs are installed by the user, never toggled by an env flag.
21. **Release order.** Parts 2, 4, 5 first (no rights risk) → part 1 after the Quranic review sheet is complete → part 3 after snapshot reachable and decisions 6–7 settled.
